AQA 7408 · 3.8.1.5 · closest approach, electron diffraction, R = R₀A1/3
Head-on, all the alpha's kinetic energy becomes electric potential energy at the closest approach: Ek = Qq/(4πε₀d), with Q = Ze and q = 2e. This gives an upper limit for the radius: the alpha never touches the nucleus.
Electrons are leptons, so they feel no strong force: they probe the charge (protons) only. At these energies they are relativistic, so λ ≈ hc/E. The first minimum is at sin θ ≈ 0.61λ/R.
R ∝ A1/3 means R³ ∝ A, so volume ∝ number of nucleons. Each nucleon takes up the same volume: nuclear density is the same for all nuclei, about 1017 kg m−3.
