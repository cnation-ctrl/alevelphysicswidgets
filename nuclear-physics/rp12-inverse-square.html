AQA 7408 · Required practical 12 · γ intensity against distance

A sealed γ source sits in its holder. You can only measure d, from the front of the holder to the GM tube window. The radioactive material is a small, unknown distance e behind the front.

d / cmcountst / sC / s−1C − B / s−1(C − B)−1/2 / s1/2
I = k/x² with x = d + e, so (C − B) = k/(d + e)². Rearranged: (C − B)−1/2 = d/√k + e/√k. A straight line: gradient 1/√k, and the line cuts the d-axis at d = −e.
