Head-on elastic collision of a neutron (mass m) with a nucleus (mass Am) at rest. Momentum and kinetic energy are both conserved, giving vafter = −v(A − 1)/(A + 1). The neutron keeps a fraction ((A − 1)/(A + 1))² of its kinetic energy. The closer the moderator's mass is to the neutron's, the more energy is transferred per collision.
