Rules of thumb. α: stopped by paper or a few cm of air, strongly ionising. β−: range about 1 m in air, stopped by about 3 mm of aluminium. γ: intensity halves every 1 cm or so of lead; never completely stopped; range in air limited only by the inverse-square law.

A beta source sits under a sheet of aluminium foil on the production line. A detector above measures the count rate. If the foil gets thicker, fewer betas get through, and the rollers squeeze harder.
