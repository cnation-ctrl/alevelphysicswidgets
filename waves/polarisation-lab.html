In an EM wave it is the electric and magnetic fields that oscillate, at right angles to each other and to the direction of travel. All EM waves travel at c = 3.00 × 108 m s−1 in a vacuum.

The highlighted particles show that each particle only oscillates about a fixed point: the energy moves, the medium does not.

Brightness reaching the eye (full bar = the lamp)

Unpolarised light oscillates in all planes perpendicular to its direction. A polarising filter only lets through the oscillations in one plane. Crossed filters (90° apart) block the light completely.

Signal strength

Radio and TV signals are plane polarised by the transmitting aerial. The receiving aerial (its rods) must be aligned with the plane of polarisation for the strongest signal. Neighbouring transmitters can use different polarisations to reduce interference.
