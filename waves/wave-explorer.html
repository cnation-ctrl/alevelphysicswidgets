Watch the orange particle: it only moves up and down about its rest position. The wave (and its energy) moves along. One full oscillation of the particle takes one period T, and in that time the wave moves forward one wavelength λ.

Displacement against time (one particle)

The two graphs look alike, so always read the x-axis label. On a displacement–distance graph, one full cycle is the wavelength λ. On a displacement–time graph, one full cycle is the period T, and f = 1/T. The amplitude is measured from the equilibrium line to a peak, not peak to trough.
