Principle of superposition: where two waves meet, the resultant displacement is the sum of the individual displacements. Two waves of the same frequency (and similar amplitude) travelling in opposite directions superpose to form a stationary wave.

Use “Step T/8” and compare: at t = 0 the waves add (antinodes at maximum); at t = T/4 they cancel everywhere (the string is flat for an instant); at nodes they always cancel.

In a stationary wave, all points between two adjacent nodes are in phase (they move up and down together) but have different amplitudes. Points in neighbouring loops (separated by an odd number of nodes) are in antiphase.
