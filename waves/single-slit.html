Single-Slit Diffraction

AQA 7408 · 3.3.2.2 · diffraction

The intensity sketch is for interest: AQA does not require the graph of intensity against angle, but you must describe the pattern.

Describe the pattern (monochromatic): a bright central maximum, twice as wide as the other maxima; dimmer side maxima of equal width that get less intense further out; dark fringes between.
White light: a white central maximum; side maxima are spectra with violet nearest the centre and red furthest out.
Diffraction is the spreading of a wave as it passes through a gap or around an obstacle. Spreading is greatest when the gap is about the same size as the wavelength. The wavelength does not change on passing through the gap.
