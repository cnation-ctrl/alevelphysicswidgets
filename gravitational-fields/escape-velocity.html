Launch Lab: Orbits and Escape

AQA 7408 · 3.7.2.4 orbital energy and escape velocity

A projectile is launched horizontally from the top of a very tall tower, 1000 km above the Earth's surface (r = 7.37 × 106 m). Air resistance is ignored.

Per kilogram at launch: Ek = ½v2, Ep = −GM/r. Total < 0: bound (falls back or orbits). Total ≥ 0: escapes.

½mv2 = GMm/R  ⇒  vesc = √(2GM/R)

The mass m of the object cancels, so every object needs the same escape speed from a given body. In practice rockets don't need to reach it at launch: they keep burning fuel.
