AQA 7408 · 3.7.2.3 g = −ΔV/Δr and area under g–r

Earth: M = 5.97 × 1024 kg, R = 6.37 × 106 m. Move the tangent point along the V–r curve.

g = −ΔV / Δr

The V–r graph gets less steep further out, so g gets smaller. The minus sign: V increases (towards zero) as r increases, but g points inwards, towards decreasing r.

Choose two distances. The shaded area under the g–r graph between them is the potential difference ΔV.
