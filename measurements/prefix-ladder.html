Where the conversions come from

1 eV = energy gained by one electron through 1 V
E = QV = (1.60×10−19 C)(1 V) = 1.60×10−19 J
1 kW h = (1000 W)(3600 s) = 3.6×106 J

e = 1.60×10−19 C, as in the AQA Data and Formulae booklet. Watts are J s−1, so the hours must go into seconds.
