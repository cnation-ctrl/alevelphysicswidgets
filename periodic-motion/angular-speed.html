Every point on the disc has the same angular speed ω: it sweeps the same angle each second. Points further out have a larger linear speed v = ωr, because they travel further round a bigger circle in the same time.
One radian is the angle at the centre when the arc length equals the radius. So θ (in rad) = arc length ÷ radius = s/r. A full circle has arc length 2πr, so 360° = 2π rad.
