AQA 7408 · 3.6.1.2 · x = A cos ωt, v and a
The shadow of a point moving round a circle at constant ω moves with SHM. Its displacement is x = A cos ωt (the clock starts at x = +A). It is fastest at the centre (vmax = ωA) and stops at the ends; the acceleration is greatest at the ends (amax = ω²A) and zero at the centre.
x = A cos ωt
v = ±ω√(A² − x²)
a = −ω²x
Calculator in radians! In x = A cos ωt the angle ωt is in radians. Using degree mode is the most common error in this topic. vmax = ωA (at x = 0) and amax = ω²A (at x = ±A) both follow from the equations above.

ω = 2πf   vmax = ωA   amax = ω²A
