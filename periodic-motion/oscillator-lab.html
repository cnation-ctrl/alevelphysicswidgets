AQA 7408 · 3.6.1.3 · simple harmonic systems
T = 2π√(m/k). The period depends only on the mass and the stiffness. It does not depend on the amplitude or on g: change them and watch T stay the same. Gravity only moves the equilibrium position (extension mg/k).
T = 2π√(l/g) uses the small-angle approximation sin θ ≈ θ (θ in radians). Below about 10° the error is under 0.2%. Push the release angle up and compare the formula with the true period. The mass of the bob makes no difference.

T = 2π√(m/k)    T = 2π√(l/g)

Answers to 3 significant figures. g = 9.81 N kg−1 unless stated.
