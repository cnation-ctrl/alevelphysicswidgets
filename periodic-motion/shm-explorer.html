AQA 7408 · 3.6.1.2 · the condition for SHM
SHM needs a restoring force proportional to displacement and always towards the equilibrium position. Then a = −(k/m)x, which has the form a = −ω²x. The minus sign means a and x are always in opposite directions. The a–x graph is a straight line through the origin with gradient −ω².
Test for SHM: is the a–x graph a straight line through the origin with a negative gradient? Repetitive motion is not enough: a bouncing ball is periodic but its acceleration is g downwards almost all the time, so it is not SHM.

a = −ω²x    ω = 2πf = 2π/T

Answers to 3 significant figures. Keep the sign of a when the question asks for acceleration.
