Current is the rate of flow of charge: 1 A = 1 C s−1. In a metal the charge carriers are free electrons, moving the opposite way to the conventional current arrow. Each electron carries e = 1.60 × 10−19 C.
Potential difference is the energy transferred per unit charge between two points: 1 V = 1 J C−1. Resistance is defined by R = V/I and is measured in ohms (Ω = V A−1).
