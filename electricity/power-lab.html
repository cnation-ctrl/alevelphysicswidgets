AQA 7408 · 3.5.1.4 · P = IV = I²R = V²/R, E = IVt
All three power equations give the same answer. Pick the one that uses the quantities you know. Double V across a fixed resistor and I doubles too, so P goes up four times (P = V²/R).
For a fixed power P = IV, a higher voltage means a smaller current. The heating loss in the cables is I²R, so ten times the voltage gives one hundredth of the loss. (At 230 V the current needed is so large the "loss" exceeds the power sent: impossible in practice.)

P = IV = I²R = V²/R    E = IVt

1 kWh = 3.6 × 106 J. Answers to 3 significant figures.
