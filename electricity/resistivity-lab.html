AQA 7408 · 3.5.1.3 · resistivity ρ = RA/L
Resistivity is a property of the material (and its temperature), not of the shape. R = ρL/A: double the length, double R. Double the diameter and the area is four times bigger, so R is a quarter. Always convert d to metres and use A = πd²/4.

R = ρL/A    A = πd²/4

Work out the factor first: what happens to L, and what happens to A? Then R ∝ L/A.

ρ = RA/L    A = πd²/4

Convert mm to m first (1 mm = 10−3 m). Answers to 3 significant figures; you may type standard form as 4.9e-7.
