RP5 Virtual Practical: Resistivity of a Wire

AQA 7408 · Required practical 5 · 3.5.1.3

Take readings at several places along the wire and in different orientations (the wire may not be perfectly round). Check the zero reading first and correct for any zero error. Resolution of the micrometer: 0.01 mm.
L / mV / VI / AR / Ω
Keep the current small and open the switch between readings so the wire does not heat up: resistivity depends on temperature. Contact resistance at the clips adds the same extra resistance to every reading.
Plot R against L. R = (ρ/A)L + contact resistance, so the gradient is ρ/A and ρ = gradient × A. The intercept is the contact resistance, which does not affect the gradient: that is why the graph method is better than one reading.
