EMF and Internal Resistance Lab

AQA 7408 · 3.5.1.6 · ε = I(R + r)
emf = energy given to each coulomb by the cell. Some is used up inside the cell (lost volts, Ir); the rest is the terminal pd V = ε − Ir. With no current, V = ε: that is how you measure emf with a high-resistance voltmeter.
Stretch: the power delivered to the load, P = I²R = ε²R/(R + r)², is a maximum when R = r. At that point half the energy is wasted inside the cell. Car batteries need a very small r because the starter motor needs a huge current.
