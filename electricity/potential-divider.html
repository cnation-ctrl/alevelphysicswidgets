AQA 7408 · 3.5.1.5 · potential divider
The pd is shared in the ratio of the resistances: Vout/Vin = R₂/(R₁ + R₂). A load in parallel with R₂ lowers the resistance of the bottom section, so Vout drops, unless RL ≫ R₂.
A variable resistor wired as a potentiometer gives an output that can be set anywhere from 0 V to the full supply pd. A variable resistor in series (a rheostat) cannot reach 0 V. This is why the I–V circuit in Lesson 2 uses a potential divider.

Vout = Vin × R₂/(R₁ + R₂)

R₂ is the resistor the output is taken across. Answers to 3 significant figures.
