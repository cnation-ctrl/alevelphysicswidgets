The voltmeter measures the terminal pd of the cell. Open the switch between readings so the cell does not run down or warm up (r changes). The protective resistor stops a dangerously large current when R = 0, and is not part of r because the voltmeter is across the cell only.
V = −rI + ε has the form y = mx + c. Plot V (y-axis) against I (x-axis): the y-intercept is ε and the gradient is −r. Extend the line to I = 0 to find ε.
