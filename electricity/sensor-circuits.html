AQA 7408 · 3.5.1.5 · potential dividers with LDRs and thermistors
Ask two questions: (1) does the sensor's resistance go up or down? (2) is the sensor across the output or not? The bigger resistor takes the bigger share of the pd. Change the fixed resistor to move the point where the device switches.

Vout = Vin × R₂/(R₁ + R₂)

LDR: R falls as light increases. NTC thermistor: R falls as temperature increases. Supply 9.0 V unless stated. Answers to 3 s.f.
