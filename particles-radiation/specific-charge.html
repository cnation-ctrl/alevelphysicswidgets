Atom Builder and Specific Charge

AQA 7408 · 3.2.1.1 · constituents of the atom
protonneutronelectron
Specific charge = charge ÷ mass, unit C kg−1. Use the magnitude of the charge and the mass of the whole particle. A neutral atom or a neutron has specific charge 0.

Logarithmic scale: each grid line is ten times the one below. The electron's specific charge is about 1800 times the proton's.
