Electron Collisions and the eV

AQA 7408 · 3.2.2.2 · ionisation, excitation and the electron volt

An electron accelerated through a p.d. of V volts gains V electronvolts: accelerating p.d. = V

Fire an electron at a mercury atom in its ground state.
1 eV is the energy transferred when one electron moves through a potential difference of 1 V: 1 eV = 1.60 × 10−19 C × 1 V = 1.60 × 10−19 J.
eV → J: multiply by 1.60 × 10−19
J → eV: divide by 1.60 × 10−19
MeV → J: multiply by 1.60 × 10−13

Check: an energy in J should be a tiny number; the same energy in eV is a sensible one (a few eV for atoms, MeV for nuclei).
