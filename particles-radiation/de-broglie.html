Electron Diffraction Lab

AQA 7408 · 3.2.2.4 · wave–particle duality
Electrons pass through a thin film of graphite. The atoms are about 10−10 m apart, similar to the electrons' de Broglie wavelength, so the electrons diffract and interfere, giving rings. Higher p.d. → faster electrons → greater momentum → shorter wavelength → less diffraction → smaller rings.
