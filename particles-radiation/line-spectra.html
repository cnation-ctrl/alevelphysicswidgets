Click a level to start, then a second level to finish. Downward = emission; upward = absorption.

Atoms can only have discrete energies. A transition between two levels emits or absorbs one photon with hf = E1 − E2. Only certain photon energies are possible, so the spectrum is a set of lines, not a continuous rainbow.
