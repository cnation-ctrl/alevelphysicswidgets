AQA 7408 · 3.7.3.2 electric field strength

Drag any charge. Drag the small yellow test charge (+1 nC) to measure E. The picture is 40 cm wide.

Rules for drawing field lines
  • Lines start on positive charges and end on negative charges (or go to infinity).
  • The arrow shows the force on a small positive test charge.
  • Lines never cross: the field has one direction at each point.
  • Closer lines mean a stronger field. Lines meet a conductor at 90°.
  • The number of lines is proportional to the size of the charge.
