AQA 7408 · 3.7.3.2 E = V/d and Fd = QΔV

The field is the same everywhere between the plates, so the force and acceleration are constant. The speed gained depends only on V, not on d.

V against x (straight line)E against x (constant)

A charged oil drop sits between horizontal plates 1.5 cm apart. Adjust V until the drop hangs still: then the electric force QE balances the weight mg.

When balanced: QV/d = mg, so Q = mgd / V. Every drop's charge comes out as a whole number of e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
