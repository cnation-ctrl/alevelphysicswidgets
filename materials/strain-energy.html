AQA 7408 · 3.4.2.1 energy stored = ½FΔL = area under the force–extension graph
Work done = average force × distance. The car's kinetic energy is transferred by plastic deformation of the crumple zone. Doubling the crumple distance halves the average force on the occupants. This is why engineers design cars to deform, and why it raises ethical trade-offs (mass, cost, protecting pedestrians as well as occupants).
