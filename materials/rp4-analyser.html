AQA 7408 · Required practical 4: determination of the Young modulus by a simple method

Measure the copper wire's diameter with a micrometer at different points along it and in different orientations. First check the zero: close the jaws with nothing between them.

#scale reading / mmcorrected d / mm

Original length L = 2.500 ± 0.005 m (tape measure, clamp to marker). A 0.10 kg hanger is already on to straighten the wire; extensions are measured from there.

added mass / kgF / NΔL loading / mmΔL unloading / mm

Add masses up to 1.2 kg, then remove them and record again. If the unloading readings do not return to zero, the wire went past its elastic limit.

Plot F against ΔL. In the straight-line region, gradient = F/ΔL, so E = gradient × L / A. Draw the steepest and shallowest lines through the error bars to get the uncertainty in the gradient. Percentage uncertainties add, and the diameter's counts twice because A ∝ d².
