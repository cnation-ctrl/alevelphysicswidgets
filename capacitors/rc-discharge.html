AQA 7408 · 3.7.4.4 discharge, time constant RC, T½ = 0.69RC
time constant RC half-time T½ = 0.69RC t V = V₀e−t/RC Q = Q₀e−t/RC I = I₀e−t/RC fraction left
The current depends on the pd, and the pd depends on the charge left. So the rate of loss of charge is proportional to the charge remaining: equal times remove equal fractions. That is exponential decay, just like radioactive decay (λ ↔ 1/RC).
