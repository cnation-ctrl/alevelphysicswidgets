AQA 7408 · Required practical 9 · log-linear plot to find RC

A capacitor labelled with its nominal value (tolerance ±20%) discharges through a known resistor. Take readings, plot ln V against t and find the real capacitance.

Supply ε = 6.00 V. Voltmeter reads to 0.01 V.

t / sV / Vln (V / V)
V = V₀e−t/RC so ln V = ln V₀ − t/RC. Compare with y = mx + c: plotting ln V against t gives a straight line with gradient −1/RC and intercept ln V₀. For charging, ln(ε − V) = ln ε − t/RC gives the same gradient.
