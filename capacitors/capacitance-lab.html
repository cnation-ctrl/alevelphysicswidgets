AQA 7408 · 3.7.4.1 capacitance, C = Q/V

Connect a capacitor to a supply. Electrons are pushed onto one plate and pulled off the other. No charge crosses the gap.

your capacitor100 μF and 1000 μF for comparison
Q against V is a straight line through the origin. The gradient is the capacitance: C = Q/V. One farad is one coulomb per volt (1 F = 1 C V−1).
