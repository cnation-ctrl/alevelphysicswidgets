Energy Store

AQA 7408 · 3.7.4.3 energy stored, E = ½QV
Each small charge ΔQ is pushed on against the pd already there, so the work done is V ΔQ: a thin strip under the graph. Adding all the strips gives the area of the triangle, E = ½QV. The average pd during charging is V/2, not V.

A battery of emf ε charges an uncharged capacitor through a resistor R. Watch where the energy goes.

time t capacitor pd energy from battery εQ energy stored ½QV heat in resistor
When fully charged, exactly half the energy from the battery is stored. The other half is dissipated in the resistance, whatever the value of R. A bigger R only makes it take longer.
