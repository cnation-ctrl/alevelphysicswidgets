AQA 7408 · 3.7.5.3 Φ = BA, flux linkage NΦ = BAN cos θ
field lines Bcoil (seen edge-on)normal to the coil
Watch the angle. θ in NΦ = BAN cos θ is measured between the field and the normal to the coil. When the plane of the coil is at right angles to B, θ = 0 and the flux linkage is a maximum. When the plane of the coil is parallel to B, θ = 90° and no flux links the coil.

If a question gives the angle between B and the plane of the coil (call it α), use sin α instead, because α = 90° − θ.

Units: Φ in webers (Wb = T m²); flux linkage NΦ in Wb-turns (often written Wb).
