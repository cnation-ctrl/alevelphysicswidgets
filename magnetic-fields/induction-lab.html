AQA 7408 · 3.7.5.4 Faraday's law ε = N ΔΦ/Δt, Lenz's law, a conductor moving in a field
Faraday: the induced emf equals the rate of change of flux linkage, ε = N ΔΦ/Δt (the gradient of the NΦ–t graph). Lenz: the induced current flows so as to oppose the change that causes it.

The emf graph is the gradient of the flux linkage graph, with a minus sign (Lenz). The area under each half of the ε–t graph equals the change in flux linkage, so the two pulses for "pass right through" have equal areas.
