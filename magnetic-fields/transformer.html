AQA 7408 · 3.7.5.6 Ns/Np = Vs/Vp, efficiency, eddy currents, power transmission at high voltage
Where the input power goes

Eddy currents: the changing flux induces currents in the core itself, which heat it. Laminating the core (thin insulated sheets) breaks up the paths and cuts them down. Copper (I²R) losses fall with thicker wire; hysteresis losses fall with a soft iron core that is easy to magnetise and demagnetise. Some flux also leaks and misses the secondary.

For the same power, a higher voltage means a smaller current (I = P/V). The power lost as heat in the cables is I²R, so ten times the voltage gives one hundredth of the loss.
