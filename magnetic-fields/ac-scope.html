rms value: the steady dc value that would give the same heating effect in a resistor. For a sine wave Vrms = V₀ ÷ √2 and Irms = I₀ ÷ √2. UK mains is 230 V rms, so V₀ = 325 V and peak-to-peak = 650 V.
