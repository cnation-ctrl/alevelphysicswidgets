AQA 7408 · 3.7.5.2 F = BQv, circular paths, the cyclotron
The magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so it does no work: the speed and kinetic energy stay constant. It provides the centripetal force: BQv = mv²/r, so r = mv ÷ BQ.
Each time the particle crosses the gap it gains energy QV. Its speed and radius grow, but the time for each half-turn stays the same (T = 2πm ÷ BQ), so a fixed-frequency alternating pd keeps it in step.
